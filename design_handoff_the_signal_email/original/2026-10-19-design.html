01   Talk About It
From this week's charts
Three AI assistants are in the top free apps
What it is. ChatGPT is the number 3 free app on the App Store this week. Two more AI assistants are close behind: Claude by Anthropic at number 9 and Google Gemini at number 11. Apple rates ChatGPT and Gemini 12+ and Claude 17+.
Why it matters. Teens can now ask an app almost anything and get an answer in seconds, from homework help to questions about life and faith. The answers sound confident, but an app is not a friend, a parent, or a pastor, and it can be wrong.
At home
•  Do you or your friends use AI apps? What do you usually ask them?
•  When you have a big question, who do you trust most for an answer, and why?
In youth group
•  What is the difference between getting information and getting wisdom?
•  Proverbs 2:6 says wisdom comes from the Lord. How would that change the way you use an AI app?
"For the LORD gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding." Proverbs 2:6 (ESV)
